    How to play

    Reach 400 chips to win. Bet up to half your bankroll. Hit to take a card; stand to let the dealer play. The dealer stands on 17. Double takes one final card for twice the bet; surrender loses half your bet. Both are available on the first decision. A natural blackjack pays 3:2, rounded down. Shop between rounds and use items on your turn. In Chaos mode, always check the active house rules and displayed totals.
